Math mode

This is very nitpicky, but write "raw $p$" instead of "raw p", so the $p$-value is in math mode. The same goes for $z$ instead of z in the $z$-score.

Final summary sentences

The main thing I would personally add is a final sentence after each result that makes clear what was going on.
